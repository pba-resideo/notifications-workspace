# RIQ-202 – Huka

[Jira](https://resideo.atlassian.net/browse/RIQ-202) · Feature · PI 26-D (also PI 27-A) · Status: To Do

## Summary

Support for Huka, the water leak detection device with smart shutoff, across the platform and the Pro apps during the beta pilot.

Scope:

- **Platform:** Device Services support for the beta pilot (bug fixing, advice to downstream teams), and temporary or permanent additional email recipients for home safety notifications, so alerts reach someone when the homeowner is away.
- **Pro tools:** remote Smart Shutoff on/off toggle and water pressure in metric (bar) as well as psi in the ProWeb Huka device detail, based on partner (Beagle) feedback. Also a fix for the Pro App flow for devices pre-registered in Pro Portal, so the handoff to the Pro app and FA app works for L-series devices and thermostats.

Label: `pi-26d-priority`
